# Privacy Policy for XT Translator

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-06

XT Translator (“the App”) is operated by **XT Labs / xtratoxiic** (“we,” “us,” or “our”).  
We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and handled when you use XT Translator.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

XT Translator collects **only the information necessary to provide translation functionality**.

### Information You Provide
- Text that you manually enter into the app for translation or conversation mode.

### Information We Do NOT Collect
XT Translator does **not** collect:
- Names, email addresses, or phone numbers  
- Account credentials (no accounts required)  
- Precise location data  
- Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data  
- Advertising identifiers  
- Personal identifiers for marketing or analytics  

---

## 2. How We Use Information

The text you provide is used **solely** to:
- Perform language translation
- Enable conversation-style translation features

Your text is **not** used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

- Translation history is stored **locally on your device** using secure local storage.
- We do **not** permanently store your translation content on our servers.
- You may clear your local translation history at any time through the app.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

XT Translator uses third-party services **only to process translations**.

These services may temporarily receive the text you submit **solely for translation processing**.  
No personal data is sold or shared for marketing or advertising purposes.

---

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including:
- Secure communication between the app and backend services
- Limiting data access to what is strictly necessary for translation

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

XT Translator is **not directed at children under the age of 13**.  
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.  
Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
